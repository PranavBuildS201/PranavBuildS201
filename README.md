## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering student interested in practical hardware design — from digital logic and RTL to microcontrollers and connected IoT systems.

I learn by building circuits, writing code, testing behaviour, studying waveforms, and documenting what I discover. My current priority is to strengthen my digital design and Verilog fundamentals while continuing to explore embedded systems and IoT.

- 🎯 **Primary focus:** Digital Design, Verilog HDL, and RTL fundamentals
- 🔬 **Design workflow:** Understand the specification → implement → simulate → inspect waveforms → debug
- 🧩 **Also exploring:** Microcontrollers, sensor interfacing, wireless communication, and IoT applications
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** Behavioural RTL, sequential logic, finite-state machines, and stronger testbench practices
- 🤝 **Interested in:** Engineering projects, collaborative learning, and relevant internship opportunities
